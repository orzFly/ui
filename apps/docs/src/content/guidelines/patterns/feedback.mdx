**Say a result where people will act on it. A toast for something done that needs nothing more,
an inline message beside whatever needs fixing, and a dialog only when nothing may go on until
it is answered.**

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do show={<InlineFieldError />}>
    Put a validation error under its field, mark the field `aria-invalid`, and point
    `aria-describedby` at the message.
  </Do>
  <Dont show={<ErrorAsToast />}>
    Send a validation error as a toast: it disappears before the field is fixed, and never says
    which field.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do show={<ArchiveToast />}>
    Toast "3 conversations archived" with an **Undo** action after a bulk change that can be
    reversed.
  </Do>
  <Dont show={<SuccessDialog />}>
    Open a dialog to say that something worked. A dialog stops everything; success should not.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Which one

| What happened | Needs people to | Use |
|---|---|---|
| An action finished as expected | Nothing; maybe undo it | `toast()`, `toast.success()`, with an `action` to undo |
| Something finished in the background, out of view | Know, maybe open it | `toast()` with an action that opens it |
| A field or a form is wrong | Fix that field | An inline message under the field |
| A surface failed to load and has nothing to show | Retry | `LoadFailure` in its place |
| A refresh failed but older data is still shown | Know the data is stale, retry | `RefreshFailure` above the data |
| An action failed and the work is not saved | Decide what to do with it | `toast.error()` with a retry, or inline if the place is on screen |
| Nothing may continue until a question is answered | Choose | `Dialog`, see [Overlays](/guidelines/patterns/overlays/) |

<Example of={ConversationsFailed} />

Mail's list failed to load and has nothing to show, so `LoadFailure` takes its place until
**Retry** brings the conversations.

A message that stays has to be removable by fixing its cause, not by a close button; one that
leaves by itself must not carry the only copy of something people need to read.

## Writing the message

- Say what happened in the past tense, naming the thing: "Profile saved", "Couldn't load the
  conversations". Do not open with "Success!" or "Error:"; the icon already says which.
- An error says what failed and, when it is known, the server's own words: `LoadFailure` takes
  them as `detail`.
- A message for one item names the item; a message for several counts them.

## On each platform

### Desktop & pointer

- Toasts stack in a corner, out of the way of the work. Hovering one pauses its timer, so it can be
  read and its action taken.
- A toast's action is reachable from the keyboard; still, anything that matters is also reachable
  where it happened, since a toast may be gone before focus reaches it.

### Touch

- A toast covers part of a small screen. Keep it to one line, and swipe it away to dismiss.
- An inline error must stay visible above the on-screen keyboard: put it directly under the field,
  not at the top of the form.
