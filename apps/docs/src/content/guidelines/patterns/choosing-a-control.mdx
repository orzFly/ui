**A Switch acts the moment it flips. A Checkbox waits for a submit, or picks items from a set.
Exactly one of a few is a RadioGroup; exactly one of many is a Select.**

The editor settings on the left apply as they change, so they are Switches, and the one with too
many languages to show at once is a Select. The status filter on the right does nothing until
**Apply**, so it is a list of Checkboxes.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do show={<SwitchesApplyNow />}>
    Use a Switch for a setting that applies immediately, with no Save button after it.
  </Do>
  <Dont show={<SwitchesInAForm />}>
    Put Switches in a form with a Save button: nobody can tell whether a flip already took effect.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    Show two to five options as a RadioGroup when people should compare them before choosing.
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    Hide three options in a Select to save a few lines: the choice costs a tap to open and the
    options cannot be read side by side.
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Which one

| The choice | Takes effect | Use |
|---|---|---|
| One setting, on or off | Immediately | `Switch` |
| One yes or no in a form, or agreeing to terms | On submit | `Checkbox` |
| Any number of items from a set: filters, facets, a bulk selection | On apply or submit | `Checkbox` list |
| Exactly one of two to five, worth seeing together | Either | `RadioGroup` |
| Exactly one of many, or of options with long names | Either | `Select` |
| A value along a range, where the exact number matters less than the feel | Either | `Slider` |
| One of a few views of the same thing | Immediately | `Tabs`: they switch what is shown, not a value that is saved |

The parent of a partly checked list is a Checkbox with `checked="indeterminate"`. A Switch has no
indeterminate state and no "applies later": if the setting only takes effect once
something else happens, it is not a Switch.

## On each platform

### Desktop & pointer

- The label is part of the hit area: pair every control with a `Label` through `htmlFor`, so a
  click on the words toggles it.
- Space toggles a Switch or Checkbox; the arrow keys move within a RadioGroup, and Tab leaves it.
- Rows stay compact. A settings list reads as a column of labels with the controls on one edge.

### Touch

- The controls take their touch sizes on a coarse pointer by themselves; there is no prop to set.
  The **Touch** button under an example previews them.
- In a settings list, put the label on the leading edge and the Switch on the trailing edge, as the
  phone's own settings do.
- A Select opens a list the width of the trigger: keep option names short enough to read in it.
